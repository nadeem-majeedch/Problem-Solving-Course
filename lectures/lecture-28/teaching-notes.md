## Quiz answer key

**A1.** When do mean and median diverge, and which survives?
- *Expected:* Under skew; the median describes the middle experience.

**A2.** Churn 10% -> 8%: both units.
- *Expected:* -2 percentage points; -20% relative.

**A3.** Price +20% then -20%: final and why.
- *Expected:* 96 - the second percentage multiplies a bigger base.

**A4.** Name three parts of an honest summary.
- *Expected:* The number with denominator, its n, and the summary choice/alternative.

**B1.** Salaries 30, 32, 35, 38, 200 (k): mean, median, and the alarm.
- *What earns marks:* Mean 67, median 35 - the gap is the skew alarm; report median or both.

**B2.** Waiting times: median 3, mean 18.5, 25% over 30 min - which single number misleads, and how?
- *What earns marks:* Any single one; the median hides the tail (ops view), the mean hides the typical user - bundle all three.

## Exit ticket - expected answers

1. When must the median replace the mean?
   - *Expected:* under skew or outliers — any time a tail can dominate

2. What is the percentage maze's core error?
   - *Expected:* comparing percentages with different bases

3. State the inspection paradox in one line.
   - *Expected:* sampled experiences oversample long waits/stays — the sampling is length-biased
