# Course Outline

Thirty-two lectures of two hours each (64 contact hours), organised in four
blocks. **Core** cases are mandatory; the case pages also contain optional
extensions. Every lecture owns its four base cases `cs-4N−3` … `cs-4N`
(lecture *N*), and fourteen lectures additionally carry one or two
**applied** cases (cs-129 – cs-144) that integrate data science, systems,
and communication scenarios — see the [case-study
catalog](../case-studies/CATALOG.md) for each lecture's full list.

Week numbers assume one lecture per week plus the scheduled lab/quiz weeks of
your calendar. The generated
[Case-Study Catalog](../case-studies/CATALOG.md) is the single source of
truth for case titles, difficulty levels, and links.

Most lectures follow the standard case-cycle rhythm; seven workshops, two
debugging clinics, one review session, and the capstone deliberately use
different structures (see [Teaching Methodology](teaching-methodology.md)).
Every plan carries prerequisite knowledge, a topic outline, worked examples,
activities, instructor notes, practice exercises, exit questions, and an
explicit learning-outcome mapping.
