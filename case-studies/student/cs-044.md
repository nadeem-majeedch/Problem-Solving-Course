## Problem

A matrix is built as [[0]*3]*2 and rows mutate together. Explain why, then build it correctly. Aliasing as the deepest beginner trap.

Your deliverable is the diagnosis of why the two rows mutate together, the corrected construction, and a written prediction of the same trap for a matrix built with append in a loop. If the problem is ambiguous anywhere,
state the assumption you are making and carry on — a stated assumption is
part of a good answer.

## Examples

- **Input:** grid = [[0]*3]*2; grid[0][0] = 9
- **Output:** both rows become [9, 0, 0] - the two rows are one object; fix: [[0]*3 for _ in range(2)]

Solve for the worked instance first. Then ask what would change if one
number, name, or condition were replaced by something stranger.

## What to notice

- Print id(grid[0]) and id(grid[1]) - identical ids convict the alias immediately.
- Slices are half-open: they make boundaries explicit instead of hidden in loop conditions.
- Aliasing is the difference between copying a list and copying a name.
