## Problem

Fill 'Dear {name}, ... {deadline} ...' templates from records, handling missing fields with a fallback. Splitting, joining, and safe replacement.

Your deliverable is the verdict for the worked instance, the normalisation rules you applied, and one input that exposes a missing rule. If the problem is ambiguous anywhere,
state the assumption you are making and carry on — a stated assumption is
part of a good answer.

## Examples

- **Input:** template "Dear {name}, your {item} is due {deadline}."; record missing "item"
- **Output:** "Dear Ada, your [item] is due Friday." with a visible fallback marker

Solve for the worked instance first. Then ask what would change if one
number, name, or condition were replaced by something stranger.

## What to notice

- Split on '{' and '}', replace known fields, mark unknown ones - never crash on missing data.
- Most string tasks are: normalise, then a simple check.
- Index arithmetic is where string bugs live — guard the ends first.
