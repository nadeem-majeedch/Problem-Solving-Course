## Problem

Rotate a list by k positions (left or right) without extra library calls. Handle k larger than the length; connect rotation to slicing.

Your deliverable is the rearranged list for the worked instance, the slice arithmetic, and a note on what aliasing would change. If the problem is ambiguous anywhere,
state the assumption you are making and carry on — a stated assumption is
part of a good answer.

## Examples

- **Input:** [1, 2, 3, 4, 5], rotate left by 2; rotate right by 2
- **Output:** left 2: [3, 4, 5, 1, 2]; right 2: [4, 5, 1, 2, 3]

Solve for the worked instance first. Then ask what would change if one
number, name, or condition were replaced by something stranger.

## What to notice

- Right rotation by k is left rotation by n-k. One slice helper, two calls.
- Slices are half-open: they make boundaries explicit instead of hidden in loop conditions.
- Aliasing is the difference between copying a list and copying a name.
