## Problem

Two nested loops with a conditional print. Predict the exact output pattern; then generalise to n lines. Pure trace discipline.

Your deliverable is the exact 4-row pattern, the loop bounds that produce it, and one variant of the inner rule (e.g. star when col > row) that you also trace. If the problem is ambiguous anywhere,
state the assumption you are making and carry on — a stated assumption is
part of a good answer.

## Examples

- **Input:** n = 4 (rows); inner prints star when col <= row else dot
- **Output:** row1 '*'; row2 '**.'; row3 '***..'; row4 '****...' - each row adds one extra '*' and one extra '.'

Solve for the worked instance first. Then ask what would change if one
number, name, or condition were replaced by something stranger.

## What to notice

- Trace the inner loop for row 2 by hand FIRST; most wrong answers come from predicting, not tracing.
- Every aggregate here is a small machine: state + update rule.
- The initial value is part of the algorithm, not a detail.
