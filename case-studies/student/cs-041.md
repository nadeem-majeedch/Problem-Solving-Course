## Problem

Move the last k people of a queue to the front, preserving order. Slicing plus concatenation; discuss negative indices.

Your deliverable is the rearranged list for the worked instance, the slice arithmetic, and a note on what aliasing would change. If the problem is ambiguous anywhere,
state the assumption you are making and carry on — a stated assumption is
part of a good answer.

## Examples

- **Input:** queue [A, B, C, D, E], k = 2 (last 2 to front)
- **Output:** [D, E, A, B, C]; k = 0 -> same; k = 5 -> same; k = 7 -> same (wrap)

Solve for the worked instance first. Then ask what would change if one
number, name, or condition were replaced by something stranger.

## What to notice

- q[-k:] + q[:-k] with k = k % n; test k=0 before celebrating (negative slice surprises).
- Slices are half-open: they make boundaries explicit instead of hidden in loop conditions.
- Aliasing is the difference between copying a list and copying a name.
