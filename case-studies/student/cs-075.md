## Problem

Rearrange a list so evens precede odds, in-place. Two pointers from both ends with a loop invariant stated before code. Termination argument required.

Your deliverable is the result for the worked instance, the invariant in one sentence, and the termination argument. If the problem is ambiguous anywhere,
state the assumption you are making and carry on — a stated assumption is
part of a good answer.

## Examples

- **Input:** [3, 1, 6, 2, 5] -> evens first: [6, 2, 3, 1, 5] (order within groups free)
- **Output:** in-place two-pointer partition; invariant: [0,l) even, (r,n-1] odd; terminates because the gap shrinks

Solve for the worked instance first. Then ask what would change if one
number, name, or condition were replaced by something stranger.

## What to notice

- l advances past evens, r retreats past odds; when both stuck, swap and repeat - until l and r cross.
- The invariant is the algorithm; the pointers are just bookkeeping.
- If you cannot say what strictly shrinks, the loop can spin forever.
