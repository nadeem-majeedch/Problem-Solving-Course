## Problem

Read numbers until a sentinel 0, then output how many were read, their sum, and the largest. First translation from pseudocode to real Python.

Your deliverable is the computed aggregates for the worked instance, the accumulator update rules, and one test input that breaks a careless initialisation. If the problem is ambiguous anywhere,
state the assumption you are making and carry on — a stated assumption is
part of a good answer.

## Examples

- **Input:** input numbers: 4, 9, 2, 9, 0 (sentinel)
- **Output:** count 4, sum 24, largest 9

Solve for the worked instance first. Then ask what would change if one
number, name, or condition were replaced by something stranger.

## What to notice

- Three state variables updated in one loop; largest must start from the first number, not 0.
- Every aggregate here is a small machine: state + update rule.
- The initial value is part of the algorithm, not a detail.

## Discussion starter

After the solution is revealed, the class spends five minutes on this
prompt: **"Which accumulator was hardest to get right, and what test would have caught the mistake?"**
